Revise Workflow

Iterate on existing story content — tighten prose, deepen layers, enhance memorable moments, fix structural issues.

Purpose

Take existing written chapters and improve them. This could mean fixing a single paragraph or restructuring an entire act. The revision process uses the Story Bible as the source of truth for what each section should accomplish.

Procedure

Step : Understand the Revision Request

Determine what kind of revision is needed:

| Type | Description | Approach |
|------|------------|----------|
| Polish| Line-level prose improvement | Focus on Prose layer only |
| Deepen| Add missing layer content | Identify gaps, weave in |
| Restructure| Reorder or reimagine scenes | Revisit beat map, reorganize |
| Voice| Fix character dialogue consistency | Read character profiles, rewrite dialogue |
| Anti-cliché| Remove generic/tired language | Run full AntiCliche.md sweep |
| Figure| Add rhetorical power to key moments | Identify moments, deploy figures |

Step : Read the Current Content

Read the existing chapter/scene/passage in full. Note:
- What works well (don't break what's working)
- What feels flat, generic, or unclear
- Which layers are being served vs. neglected
- Where the pacing drags or rushes
- Dialogue voice consistency

Step : Compare Against Story Bible

Read the relevant Story Bible beats and check:
- Is the chapter accomplishing what the beat map says it should?
- Are all seven layers represented?
- Is the sacred flaw visible/pressured as planned?
- Are mystery elements properly planted/developed?
- Does the prose match the Aesthetic Profile?

Step : Execute Revision

For Polish revisions:. Go paragraph by paragraph
. Replace weak verbs with strong verbs
. Cut redundant words and sentences
. Sharpen descriptions (one perfect detail > three adequate)
. Ensure sentence length varies
. Run Anti-Cliché sweep

For Deepen revisions:. Identify which layers are underserved
. For each underserved layer, find natural insertion points
. Add layer content through action and behavior, NOT exposition
. Example: To deepen Character layer, add a moment where the sacred flaw affects a decision
. Example: To deepen Mystery, plant a detail that will matter three chapters later

For Restructure revisions:. List all scenes in current order
. For each scene, identify its primary purpose (which beat it serves)
. Reorder scenes to improve:
   - Causal chain clarity
   - Pacing rhythm (fast/slow alternation)
   - Mystery information flow
   - Emotional escalation
. Remove scenes that serve no beat
. Add scenes for beats that are missing

For Voice revisions:. Read character profiles from Story Bible
. For each character's dialogue, check:
   - Could you identify the speaker without attribution?
   - Does vocabulary match their education/background?
   - Does sentence structure match their personality?
   - Is subtext present in emotionally charged moments?
. Rewrite dialogue that fails these checks

For Anti-cliché revisions:. Run FULL sweep from `AntiCliche.md`
. Flag every instance from every Banned list
. Apply the Freshness Rules:
   - Specificity Test
   - Sensory Replacement
   - Action Test
   - Comparison Kill
   - Verb Test
   - Dialogue Voice Test
. Replace each flagged instance with fresh alternative

For Figure revisions:. Identify the -most important moments in the chapter
. Read `RhetoricalFigures.md` Figure Deployment table
. For each key moment, select appropriate figure(s)
. Rewrite the key line(s) using the selected figure(s)
. Ensure figures feel natural, not forced
. Verify the figure matches the character's voice

Step : Consistency Check

After revision, verify:
- [ ] Changes don't contradict earlier/later chapters
- [ ] Character voice remains consistent
- [ ] Mystery information state is still correct
- [ ] Causal chain is still intact
- [ ] Aesthetic Profile is maintained
- [ ] No new clichés were introduced

Step : Update Story Bible

If the revision changed anything structural:
. Update the relevant beat map entries
. Update character profiles if behavior changed
. Update mystery architecture if information flow changed
. Update ISC criteria to reflect the revision
. Note the revision in the PRD LOG

Step : Output

Present the revised content with:
. The revised prose (with changes highlighted if helpful)
. Brief summary of what changed and why
. Any Story Bible updates made
. Recommendation for next revision pass (if needed)
